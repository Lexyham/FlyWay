30+ global airports · All travel purposes · Instant booking

Travel class changes the fare, not flight time. Departure and arrival times are local to each airport; journey duration is elapsed time and includes estimated stop or layover time.

⚡ Popular Routes:
Non-stop Flight
The fastest option, with no stops. Its estimated journey time is based on route distance and includes a short ground-time allowance.
Direct Flight
A single flight number that may stop for passengers or refuel, but you usually stay on the same plane. Estimates allow about 45 extra minutes for the stop.
Connecting Flight
At least two separate planes and flight numbers. You change planes during a layover; estimates allow about 2.5 extra hours. Cabin class changes price, not travel time.
